2026.07.21

Announcement of Phased High Tariffs on Imported Generic Drugs

  •  Imported generic drugs will remain tariff-free for two years, followed by a phased tariff schedule of 100% for one year and 200% thereafter
  •  The measure is intended to encourage the reshoring of generic drug manufacturing to the United States, where generic medicines account for more than 90% of all prescriptions dispensed
  •  Existing policies for patented drugs, branded pharmaceuticals, and innovative medicines will remain unchanged
  •  Effective Aug 1, with a two-year tariff exemption, followed by a 100% tariff in 2028 and a 200% tariff from 2029 onward

2026.02.20

U.S. Supreme Court Rules IEEPA Tariffs Unlawful and Invalid

  •  The Court ruled (6–3) that the authority to impose tariffs is a power exclusively vested in Congress, and that tariffs imposed under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA) are unlawful
  •  Accordingly, the reciprocal tariffs imposed on individual countries, as well as the fentanyl-related tariffs imposed on China, Mexico, and Canada, are invalidated
  •  Product-specific tariffs imposed under Section 232 of the Trade Expansion Act remain in effect and are unaffected by this ruling

  • Refrigerators and freezers, which had already been added to steel derivatives, are now also included under aluminum derivatives,
    A wide range of automotive parts, such as engines and other components (previously not covered under automotive tariffs), have been newly added to steel derivative products

  • Effective Aug 18 (no exceptions for items shipped prior to Aug 18)
