Global supply chains are undergoing unprecedented transformation. Driven by AI, digital technologies, and geopolitical uncertainty, businesses must build more resilient, intelligent, and sustainable supply chains.

At the same time, companies operating in Europe, particularly in Hungary, are navigating increasingly stringent environmental regulations and evolving policy landscapes, requiring more agile and proactive supply chain strategies.

The Samsung SDS Cello Square Conference Hungary 2026 brings together industry experts and business leaders to explore the AI-driven logistics megatrends shaping the future of supply chains and to share practical insights into building smarter, more resilient, and sustainable logistics operations.

Join Samsung SDS to discover how intelligent logistics and digital innovation can help your business navigate change and unlock new opportunities for growth.
