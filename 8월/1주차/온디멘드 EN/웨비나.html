2026 Logistics Market Review for H1 & Outlook for H2

The first half of 2026 was shaped by significant geopolitical disruptions, most notably the conflict in the Middle East and the closure of the Strait of Hormuz, both of which had a profound impact on global logistics markets and supply chains.

In ocean logistics, tariff uncertainty and concerns over rising fuel costs prompted widespread front-loading of shipments, resulting in an earlier-than-usual peak shipping season.

At the same time, continued disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz and the Red Sea tightened effective shipping capacity, keeping the market supply-constrained and driving freight rates sharply higher across all major trade lanes.

Air logistics also experienced exceptional market strength. Capacity disruptions originating from the Middle East, combined with strong demand from AI infrastructure and semiconductor supply chains, pushed air freight rates to their highest levels in the past three years.

During this webinar, Samsung SDS's Ocean and Air Logistics Pricing experts will provide a data-driven review of the key market developments that shaped the first half of 2026.

They will also share their outlook for the second half of the year, examining market fundamentals, supply-demand dynamics, and the critical risks that global shippers should continue to monitor.

Agenda

Agenda
I. 2026 H1 Logistics Market Review
  • Ocean : Capacity remained constrained amid disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz and the Red Sea
  • Air : Air freight rates surged as Middle East-related capacity disruptions coincided with strong AI infrastructure and semiconductor demand.
Agenda
II. 2026 H2 Logistics Market Outlook
  • Ocean : Freight rates expected to remain elevated before easing in Q4 as front-loading effects fade and new vessel deliveries improve capacity fundamentals.
  • Air : Demand growth shifting from cross-border e-commerce to AI Infrastructure and semiconductor supply chains.
