등록일2025-03-26

Cargo Insights spoke with Hitendra Mankani, Regional Director for the MEIA Region at Samsung SDS, about how global shippers can turn regulatory and geopolitical pressure into an advantage. He argues that compliance has moved out of the back office and into supply chain strategy, and that digital platforms, standardized data and real-time visibility are what keep it from becoming a source of delay. He also points to end-to-end visibility — digital control towers and predictive analytics — as the reason some companies reroute shipments and rebalance inventory far faster than others, and calls for diversified sourcing, supplier ecosystem development and multi-tier visibility as the foundation of long-term resilience.
