Cello Square Conference 2026 Mexico: Innovation, AI, and Nearshoring Drive a New Era of Logistics

Samsung SDS held a new edition of the Cello Square Conference 2026, bringing together business leaders, logistics experts, and representatives from the economic sector to discuss the key trends transforming global supply chains.

Welcome Message

Prior to the start of the event, Samsung SDS Mexico thanked customers and partners for their participation, highlighting its continued growth and commitment to strengthening its operations and expanding its business across Mexico and Latin America.

Global Logistics Megatrends 2026

As the keynote speaker, Edward Lee presented an analysis of the current global logistics landscape and the factors directly impacting supply chains today, including geopolitical conflicts, rising tariffs, energy market volatility, and the effects of climate change.

During his presentation, he emphasized that logistics has evolved into a strategic factor for business continuity and competitiveness. He also explained how artificial intelligence is accelerating the industry's transformation through more predictive, automated, and resilient solutions.

Among the key trends shared by Samsung SDS were:

  • Integrated and trusted data platforms
  • Predictive operational visibility
  • Agentic AI applied to logistics
  • Automation of logistics operations and warehouses
  • Resilient networks powered by nearshoring
  • Supply chain cybersecurity

Lee also presented examples of Samsung SDS’s Global Control Center (GCC), a platform that enables real-time monitoring of logistics operations, early risk identification, and proactive responses to global disruptions.

A Future Driven by Innovation and Resilience

Cello Square Conference 2026 reaffirmed the importance of technology, artificial intelligence, and strategic collaboration in building more resilient, efficient, and agile supply chains capable of responding to today’s rapidly changing business environment
